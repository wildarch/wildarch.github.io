From May through July I lived in Munich for a research visit at the Technical University of Munich, where I had the pleasure of working with [Altan Birler](https://db.in.tum.de/people/sites/birler/index.shtml?lang=en), [Thomas Neumann](https://db.in.tum.de/people/sites/neumann/?lang=en) and many other excellent researchers at the Chair for Database Systems. I extended the [Umbra Database System](https://umbra-db.com) to include a highly flexible loop construct in order to natively run complex graph analytics and datalog workloads.
