I am a PhD Candidate at [Eindhoven University of Technology](https://research.tue.nl/en/persons/daan-de-graaf).
I work on support for user-defined graph algorithms in the [AvantGraph](https://avantgraph.io) database.

## News 
- *Oct'2023* Our paper [Randomized Testing of Byzantine Fault Tolerant Algorithms](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3586053) 
  was recognized with an **ACM SIGPLAN Distinguished Paper award** at [OOPSLA 2023](ttps://2023.splashcon.org/track/splash-2023-oopsla).
- *Oct'2023* We shipped the first publicly available preview of AvantGraph. 
  It is published as a docker container on our GitHub Container Registry.
  [Get started here](https://github.com/avantlab/avantgraph).
- *Sep'2023* I have started my work as a doctoral candidate at the TU/e Database group.
  I will continue my work on the graphalg language for high-performance algorithm support. 
- *Aug'2023* I defended my MSc thesis 
  [Efficient Execution of User-Provided Graph Algorithms in a Graph Database](http://resolver.tudelft.nl/uuid:1b5f0236-87f2-41f0-8af8-0911cb3b4d54), 
  and was awarded the **Cum Laude** distinction.
- *Feb'2023* Our paper 
  [Randomized Testing of Byzantine Fault Tolerant Algorithms](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3586053) 
  with [Levin N. Winter](https://dblp.org/pid/346/8791.html),

  [Klaus von Gleissenthall](https://dblp.org/pid/86/10265.html) and
